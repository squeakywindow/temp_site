How it works

Three steps to never buying a book you could borrow

1

Install the extension

Download ShelfBee from the App Store and enable it in Safari's extension settings on your iPhone, iPad, or Mac.

2

Browse like normal

Visit any book page on Amazon or Goodreads. ShelfBee automatically detects the book and checks your library.

3

Borrow or buy

A small banner shows availability status. One tap opens Libby directly so you can borrow — or you know to go ahead and buy.
